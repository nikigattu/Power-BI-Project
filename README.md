# Power-BI-Project
# Sales Performance Dashboard
 Developed an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard in Power BI to monitor key business metrics including
sales, profit, quantity sold, and customer trends.
◦ Performed data cleaning, transformation, and modeling using Power Query to improve data quality and reporting
accuracy. Created DAX measures and calculated columns to analyze revenue, profit margins, and sales growth
across different regions and product categories.
◦ Identified top-performing products, customers, and sales regions through trend and comparative analysis.
◦ Automated reporting processes and enhanced decision-making by delivering actionable business insights through
interactive visualizations.
